Who we are

Fortis ("we", "us") provides continuous security testing software for financial technology companies. For personal data about our website visitors and account users, we are the data controller. For personal data that may appear in findings from your systems, we act as your data processor under a data processing agreement.

What we collect

When you visit our website or contact us

  • Name, work email, company, role and anything you choose to tell us in a form.
  • Basic analytics about how the site is used, such as pages viewed and device type.

When you use Fortis

  • Account details: name, email, phone number for one-time passcodes, and team membership.
  • Billing details: billing contact and invoices. Card details are handled by our payment processor, not by us.
  • Finding data: rule IDs, file paths, line numbers and masked snippets sent by the CLI and staging checks. We do not receive your source code.
  • Service logs: IP addresses, timestamps and actions, for security and audit.

How we use it

  • To provide, secure and support the Fortis service.
  • To respond to enquiries and early-access requests.
  • To bill you and meet tax and accounting duties.
  • To improve our rules and product, using aggregated, de-identified data only.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use finding data to train models for other customers.

Lawful basis

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we rely on: performance of a contract (to provide the service), legitimate interests (to secure and improve it), legal obligation (for tax and records), and consent where we ask for it (for example, marketing emails, which you can withdraw at any time).

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run Fortis, such as cloud hosting, email delivery, SMS passcodes and payment processing, under contracts that require them to protect it. Our planned sub-processors are listed in the trust centre. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

Where it's stored

Fortis data is hosted in an Africa cloud region by default, with a Nigerian hosting option for Enterprise customers. Where data is transferred outside Nigeria, we use safeguards permitted by the NDPA.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To use any of these rights, email privacy@fortis.dev.

Security

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, limit staff access, and test our own systems. Read more in our trust centre.
